# Is Kalshi Lying to You?

If you recently watched _The Odyssey_ and have strong opinions, you can go to [Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxoscarpic/oscar-for-best-picture) and speculate on _how good it was_. Right now, the **_odds_** that _The Odyssey_ wins 'Best Picture' at the Oscars are ~58%. Based on that information, you follow some relatively straightforward logic:

1. If you thought the movie was phenomenal and will easily win (i.e., more than 58% chance), you should buy a `YES` contract.
2. If you thought the movie was not as great as everyone says and are quite confident that it will not win (i.e., less than 58% chance), you should buy a `NO` contract.
3. If you don't have strong opinions either way (i.e., you agree there's around a 58% chance), it doesn't make too much sense to put your money on the line

Frankly, I think these odds are way too high (I have high hopes for _Dune: Part Three_).
